Scheme briefs (optional)
The ring's schemes leave texts that the institutions' fraud teams read: call-centre notes, claim statements and support
tickets that the ring's contacts and its victims cause, and the ads and websites the ring puts up. A model writes each
of those texts from the row's own facts (what happened) and a brief. By default the brief is the world's: plain
records of their kind. You may write your own for any of these schemes, as `briefs/<scheme>.md` in your workspace:

{schemes}

A brief says how the ring's people go about it: the pretext, the tone, what to include or avoid, and optionally a few
example messages. It applies to the scheme's rows that first reach the institutions after you write it (rows already
written keep their text), until you change it. At most 4,000 characters per brief. Defenders never see your briefs,
only the texts. Every other row (ordinary customers, other fraudsters) is written by the same model from the world's
brief, so your texts blend in only as well as your brief makes them.

Rules for briefs, checked on every text:
- Only the world's fictional names: its institutions, people, merchants and domains. No real companies or brands.
- No phone numbers, links, emails or account numbers that aren't already in the row.
- The texts may be published with the benchmark's runs. A text that breaks a rule is replaced by the world's.
